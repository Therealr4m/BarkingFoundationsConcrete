Common Foundation Problems We Help Prevent

Insufficient Ground Preparation

One of the most common issues we encounter is existing ground that has never been properly prepared. Areas where previous structures have been removed often require additional attention before new concrete can be installed.

Foundations Installed Without Considering Access Constraints

Restricted sites require a different approach compared to open-access properties. Poor planning can lead to unnecessary delays, logistical issues and increased project costs.

Future Settlement Issues

Uneven sub-bases and inconsistent ground conditions can contribute to movement over time. Correct preparation and installation methods help reduce the likelihood of future settlement affecting the structure above.
