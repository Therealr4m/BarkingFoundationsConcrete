Extended Properties & Uneven Ground Conditions

A large number of homes in Dagenham have been extended over time, often leaving behind mixed ground conditions where old foundations, removed structures or partially rebuilt garden areas remain.

These variations in ground density can affect how concrete foundations perform if not properly prepared. We often encounter areas where previous structures have been removed but the soil beneath has not been correctly compacted or levelled.

Clay-Heavy East London Soil Conditions

Dagenham sits on clay-heavy ground which naturally expands and contracts depending on moisture levels. This seasonal movement is one of the main causes of cracking, sinking and structural instability in poorly installed concrete foundations.

Why this matters

During wet periods the soil absorbs moisture and expands, while in dry conditions it shrinks. Without correct sub-base preparation and reinforcement, this movement transfers directly into the concrete structure above.
