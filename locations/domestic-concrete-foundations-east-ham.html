Concrete Foundations Planned Around East Ham Homes

Domestic foundation work is rarely just a case of digging an area and filling it with concrete. The ground underneath needs to be prepared correctly for the structure being built above it.

On residential jobs, we look at the existing ground, levels, access and the proposed footprint before deciding how the work needs to be carried out. This is particularly important where previous garden works, old structures or altered ground levels have left different areas of the site.

Why ground preparation matters

Problems underneath a foundation are difficult to deal with once the concrete has been installed and the building is in place. Poor preparation can contribute to movement, sinking or cracking, which is why we do not cut corners during the groundwork stage.

The aim is to prepare the ground properly before the concrete is placed so the finished foundation is suitable for the intended domestic project.

Working Around Tight Access in East Ham

Restricted access is something we account for before starting residential foundation work. Around East Ham, narrow pathways between terraced properties can make it difficult to bring machinery, excavated material and concrete through in the same way as they would be on a larger property.

We look at the route from the front of the property through to the working area and plan the job around what can realistically be moved through it. Where access is limited, this can influence the excavation method, material handling and concrete delivery arrangements.

Keeping the installation practical

The objective is to keep the work moving without making assumptions about access. On closely built residential streets, planning the logistics properly before the pour can save considerable time once the groundwork is underway.

Domestic Foundation Work for Residential Projects

We carry out concrete foundation installations for homeowners undertaking structural work or adding a new building to their property. The foundation is planned around the footprint and requirements of the intended structure rather than using a one-size-fits-all approach.

This can include new foundation work where an existing area of the garden needs to be developed, as well as projects where previous concrete or groundwork needs to be removed and replaced.

East Ham Ground Conditions and Foundation Preparation

Clay-heavy ground across parts of East London can respond to changes in moisture. Ground can become wetter during prolonged rainfall and dry out during warmer periods, which is one reason the preparation beneath a domestic foundation needs to be taken seriously.

We take the condition of the ground into account when preparing the foundation area. Excavation, formation levels and the supporting layers beneath the concrete are dealt with before the concrete installation takes place.

Dealing with existing ground levels

Gardens around terraced properties are not always uniform. Previous landscaping, removed structures and years of garden alterations can leave areas of different ground levels or disturbed soil.

These conditions are identified during preparation so the foundation can be installed on properly prepared ground rather than simply following the existing garden surface.

Why Homeowners in East Ham Use an Experienced Foundation Contractor

Foundation work is one part of a residential project where rushing the preparation can create problems later. Once the structure is built, correcting movement or settlement underneath it is far more disruptive than dealing with the ground properly at the beginning.

Our approach is based on dealing with the site in front of us. That means allowing for the access available at a terraced property, checking the condition of the existing ground and making sure the foundation area is properly prepared before the concrete installation.

We also understand that residential work has to be coordinated around an occupied property. Access through narrow pathways, material movement and keeping the working area controlled all form part of planning the installation.
