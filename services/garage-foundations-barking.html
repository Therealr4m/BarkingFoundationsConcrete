What Happens When Garage Foundations Are Installed Poorly?

Poor Sub-Base Leading To Sinking

The most common issue we encounter is inadequate preparation beneath the concrete. If the sub-base is not properly installed and compacted, sections of the foundation can begin to settle under load.

Cracking Concrete

Movement beneath the slab often results in cracking. While some cracks may appear minor initially, they can indicate underlying stability problems.

Garage Structural Movement

If the foundation moves, that movement can transfer directly into the garage above. This can affect walls, door openings and the overall performance of the structure.

Water Collection Around The Building

Poor drainage planning can leave standing water around the garage after rainfall, increasing maintenance issues and reducing site usability.

Garage Foundation Questions

How thick should a garage foundation be?

The required thickness depends on the garage type, loading requirements and site conditions. We assess each project individually before installation.

Do garage foundations need reinforcement?

Some garage foundations benefit from reinforcement depending on the structure being installed and the ground conditions present on site.

Can you install foundations before the garage supplier arrives?

Yes. Many customers arrange the foundation installation first so the garage can be installed directly onto the completed slab.

How long does installation take?

Most residential garage foundation projects are completed over a relatively short timeframe depending on size, excavation requirements and access conditions.

Can you work with restricted access?

Yes. Restricted side access and neighbouring boundary constraints are common considerations on residential projects and are factored into our planning.

Will the foundation be level?

Yes. Accurate levelling is one of the most important parts of the installation because the garage structure will rely directly on the foundation beneath it.
