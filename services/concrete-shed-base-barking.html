Concrete Shed Base Specialists In Barking

Our team at Concrete Foundations Barking regularly install concrete shed bases for homeowners throughout Barking. Some customers contact us before their shed arrives so the base is ready for installation, while others already have an existing shed and want to replace an ageing or inadequate foundation with a stronger long-term solution.

A properly installed concrete shed base provides a level, stable and durable foundation that helps support the weight of the structure above. Unlike paving slabs, timber frames or gravel bases, a professionally installed concrete base helps reduce movement, improves longevity and creates a solid platform designed for years of use.

The quality of the shed itself is only one part of the equation. Even a well-built shed can suffer from sticking doors, uneven floors and structural movement if the base beneath it has not been installed correctly. Ground preparation, sub-base installation, drainage planning and concrete placement all play an important role in the long-term performance of the finished base.

  • Site-specific excavation and ground preparation
  • Compacted sub-base installation for long-term stability
  • Level concrete placement designed for shed installations
  • Drainage consideration to help minimise future water issues
  • Reinforcement options where additional strength is required

Every concrete shed base we install is planned around the specific requirements of the project. Our goal is simple: provide homeowners with a stable, durable and professionally installed foundation that gives their shed the support it needs for long-term performance.

Why A Concrete Shed Base Is Usually The Best Option

Long-Term Structural Stability

Unlike timber bases and lightweight alternatives, a concrete shed base provides permanent structural support capable of handling significant weight without sinking or shifting. This makes it ideal for sheds of all sizes from a small 4'x4' shed to larger timber sheds.

Level Surface Installation

One of the biggest causes of shed problems is an uneven foundation. We carefully prepare, level and install each concrete base to provide a flat surface that supports proper shed construction and long-term performance.

Reduced Maintenance

Once installed correctly, concrete shed bases require very little maintenance compared with alternative base systems. This makes them a cost-effective long-term solution for homeowners investing in garden structures.

What Makes A Good Shed Base?

A shed base is far more than simply pouring concrete into a hole. The long-term performance of the structure depends heavily on excavation depth, sub-base preparation, drainage considerations and concrete thickness.

We approach every concrete shed base installation as a structural groundwork project. Correct preparation beneath the concrete is often just as important as the concrete itself. By ensuring the ground is properly excavated, compacted and stabilised before pouring begins, we help create a base that remains level and stable for years.

Ground Preparation

Weak or poorly compacted ground is one of the leading causes of shed base movement. We install compacted sub-base systems designed to improve stability beneath the concrete and minimise future settlement.

Drainage Considerations

Water retention around sheds can cause ongoing issues if the installation is not planned correctly. We assess drainage requirements during the planning stage and install bases with long-term durability in mind.

Concrete Strength & Thickness

Different shed types require different levels of support. We select installation methods based on the intended use of the shed and expected loading requirements to ensure suitable long-term performance.

Concrete Shed Bases For Different Types Of Garden sheds

Not every shed places the same demands on a foundation. The size of the structure, the materials used and how the building will be used all influence the type of concrete base required. We install concrete shed bases for a wide range of residential garden structures throughout Barking.

Timber Garden Sheds

Traditional timber sheds benefit significantly from a level concrete base. Proper support helps prevent twisting, door alignment issues and structural movement over time.

Heavy Duty Storage Sheds

Larger storage sheds often carry significant internal loads including tools, machinery and equipment. A reinforced concrete base helps distribute these loads more effectively than lightweight alternatives.

Workshop Sheds

Workshop buildings often contain benches, machinery and heavy equipment. These structures typically require a stronger and more robust foundation system capable of supporting increased weight.

Metal Sheds

Metal sheds require a flat and stable installation surface to ensure proper assembly and long-term performance. Concrete provides one of the most reliable foundation options for these structures.

Common Problems We See With Existing Shed Bases

Sinking Corners

Many older shed bases begin to sink because they were installed directly onto soft ground without sufficient preparation underneath. This can cause uneven loading throughout the structure.

Cracked Concrete

Thin concrete installations without proper preparation or reinforcement can crack as ground conditions change. In some cases complete replacement is more cost-effective than ongoing repairs.

Uneven Surfaces

Even small variations in level can create issues for shed installation. Doors may stop closing properly and wall sections can become misaligned over time.

Drainage Issues

Water collecting around the base of a shed can accelerate deterioration and affect long-term performance. Proper planning during installation helps minimise these risks.

Concrete Shed Base Questions

Do I need reinforcement in a concrete shed base?

Some installations benefit from reinforcement depending on loading requirements and site conditions. Reinforcement requirements are assessed on a project-by-project basis.

Can a shed be installed directly onto concrete?

In most cases, yes. A properly installed concrete shed base provides a stable and level surface suitable for shed installation.

How long does a concrete shed base last?

A correctly installed concrete shed base can last for decades when supported by suitable ground preparation and installation methods.

Can you remove an existing shed base?

Yes. We regularly remove failed or outdated bases before installing new concrete foundations.

How long should a concrete shed base cure?

Concrete begins setting within hours, but full strength develops over a much longer curing period. The exact timeframe depends on weather conditions and slab thickness.

Do all shed bases need reinforcement mesh?

No. Smaller sheds often do not require reinforcement, although larger or heavier buildings frequently benefit from it.
